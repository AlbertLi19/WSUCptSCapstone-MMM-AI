## Work Summary (Developer Facing)
In this sprint, our focus was establishing communication as a team with our client, setting up recurring meetings, and unraveling a year’s worth of work created by the previous Capstone team. We successfully met our fellow team members and our client, Dr. Zare, to establish strong communication and recurring meeting structures. We met with Dr. Zare to discuss the general project expectations, including work done by the previous Capstone team and our next steps for extending functionality. We received access to the existing project repository and compiled a list of research papers with a focus on computer vision and microscopic image segmentation. Since gaining access to the project, we have delved into understanding the existing pipeline from image selection to analyzed output. The largest barrier in this sprint has been understanding the trove of existing files, technologies, and documentation left to us. As a team we have begun to discuss the implementation for the first addition Dr. Zare would like us to implement; batch segmentation for a multi-image analysis. 


## Unfinished Work
Our team agreed to focus this sprint on understanding the existing project left to us by the previous Capstone team. Apart from administrative reports/documents, we have not added any new files that enhance this project. We now better understand the current image analyses and can begin technical implementations of batch segmentation and further algorithmic analysis for the next sprint cycle. We are compiling a list of detailed questions for Dr. Zare to align expectations regarding how we intend to proceed next cycle. All currently active User Stories have been added with the intent of structuring our expectations for the next cycle and were not planned for this sprint.

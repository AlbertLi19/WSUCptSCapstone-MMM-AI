## Minutes (1/24/2024)
The overall structure of the project was largely discussed. Dr. Zare would like us to analyze impact images of damaged niobium-titanium allow materials to see where points of failure occure on a macro, meso, and micro scale. The method of analyzing will involve image segmentation to highlight the pores in the damaged materials, followed by statistical analysis on those pores based on their spatial dimensions.

On top of the analysis of the damaged materials, we are also going to be identifying the impurities of the material. These are the first regions where the cracks initiate when damaged, and finding the distribution of the impurities can allow Dr. Zare to achieve a better understanding of the expected performance of the material.

Lastly, Dr. Zare shared with us a collection of images for analysis, along with a Python script that was started by one of here collaborators.
